🧪 The intelligence layer

The Lab helps you match, de-risk, and know your next step.

The Lab is iDEx's intelligence layer. It matches ideas with the right collaborators, assembles a de-risking brief from market and prior-art signals, and guides the next steps toward readiness — all with cited sources.

◇

Matching

Pairs the skill an iDeaer seeks with verified collaborators, scoring fit and explaining why — so matches are legible, not a black box.

◇

De-risking brief

Summarizes market signal, similar and prior work, and feasibility pointers — each tied to a cited source you can inspect.

◇

Next-step guidance

Suggests validation milestones and IP steps, and gauges readiness for optional investor visibility — on your schedule.

ℹ The Lab assesses credibility and readiness — it does not predict which ideas will win. Judgment stays with you and your collaborator.

Sample de-risking brief

Materials / Hardware

For the idea "A lower-cost home water-testing method" · generated inside the Collaboration Room.

Market signal

Consumer demand for at-home water-quality testing is growing alongside aging infrastructure and lead-exposure awareness. Current options split between inexpensive-but-unreliable strips and accurate-but-costly lab mail-ins, leaving an unserved middle this idea targets. ⌁ source: iDEx market index · consumer water safety · 2026 [1]

Similar / prior work

Microfluidic colorimetric assays are well represented in academic literature, but most remain lab-bound and are not optimized for low-cost consumer manufacture. A prior-art scan found no dominant consumer product combining phone-read colorimetry with a disposable low-cost strip at this price point. ⌁ source: prior-art scan · 42 references · patents + publications [2]

Feasibility pointers

  • ✓ Core chemistry validated at bench scale (120 samples vs. lab reference).
  • ✓ Phone-read calibration shifts cost from hardware to software.
  • ⚠ Reagent-film shelf life and volume supply is the key open risk to resolve early. [3]

Suggested next steps

Validation milestones

  • 1 Field pilot with 25 households.
  • 2 Independent accuracy comparison.
  • 3 Reagent stability over 12 months.

IP steps

  • 1 File a provisional before any public demo.
  • 2 Keep enabling detail room-only.
  • 3 Clear freedom-to-operate on reagent film.

Investor readiness

Readiness for optional investor visibility: Early. Clear the shelf-life risk and complete a field pilot to reach Ready.
