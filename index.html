How it works

Three steps from a sealed idea to a working collaboration.

1

Post a Teaser

Publish an abstracted, non-enabling summary. The public sees only the Teaser — your Full Detail stays sealed. A disclosure-risk guard watches for anything that could cost you patent rights.

2

Match & open a private room

The Lab surfaces collaborators who fit the skill you seek. When you both agree, a mutual NDA opens a private Collaboration Room and the Full Detail is unsealed — for the two of you only.

3

Build together, each with a Stake

Agree roles and a Stake split — equity, co-authorship, or royalties — e-sign, and build. Every disclosure and agreement is recorded with provenance.

Built to feel safe to disclose

iDEx is the opposite of a casual social app. Disclosing a patentable idea should feel deliberate, protected, and on the record.

🔒

Staged disclosure

Public Teaser now; sealed Full Detail only inside a room. You control what crosses the line, and when.

📝

NDA-gated rooms

Full Detail unseals only after both parties accept a mutual NDA. No preview, no leaks, no "just this once."

⏱

Timestamped provenance

Every disclosure and signature is recorded with a time-stamped Provenance Record you can point to later.

✓

Verified members

Identity, affiliation, and an idea-quality bar — plus reputation from real collaborations. Vetting is about credibility, not predicting which ideas win.
